RPCPPE Upload Sample (sheet-by-sheet)

IMPORTANT:
1) The importer skips rows 1 to 11.
2) Put your actual data STARTING at row 12.
3) Keep columns exactly in this order: A to N.
4) Use real dates in column K when possible.
5) Fund source goes in column L (example: 01, 05).

Column map:
A=Article
B=Description
C=Property Number
D=Unit of Measure
E=Unit Value
F=Qty per Property Card
G=Qty per Physical Count
H=Shortage/Overage Qty (optional)
I=Shortage/Overage Value (optional)
J=Remarks (optional)
K=Date Acquired (optional)
L=Fund Source (optional but recommended)
M=Accounting Value (optional)
N=Status (optional)

How to use:
- Open a sample CSV in Excel.
- Keep rows 1-11 as spacer/header area.
- Paste your rows from row 12 downward.
- Save as .xlsx and upload through RPCPPE Batches.
